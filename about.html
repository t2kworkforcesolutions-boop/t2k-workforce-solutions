About T2K

Connecting dependable workers with meaningful opportunities while helping contractors find reliable manpower.

Who We Are

T2K Workforce Solutions provides workforce and labor support for contractors, builders, property managers, and businesses throughout the Dallas–Fort Worth Metroplex.

We specialize in construction cleanup, general labor, demolition support, landscaping labor, and workforce placement. Our goal is to build reliable crews, create opportunities for hardworking individuals, and deliver professional service on every project.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: connect people, power projects, and provide dependable workforce solutions that help businesses move forward.

MEET THE FOUNDER

Taliah Busby

Founder & Owner | T2K Workforce Solutions

Taliah Busby founded T2K Workforce Solutions with a hands-on approach to construction and workforce support. T2K was built to connect contractors, businesses, and property owners with dependable workers while creating real opportunities for people who are ready to work and grow.

With experience across construction, renovation, project support, and team leadership, Taliah remains actively involved in the work behind T2K — from building client relationships and coordinating crews to helping projects get completed efficiently and professionally.
